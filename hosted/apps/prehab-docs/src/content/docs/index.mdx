---
title: TrainStack Prehab
description: Native iOS and macOS apps for a daily prehab practice.
---

Prehab combines structured onboarding, a Coach conversation, personalized plans,
exercise discovery, guided sessions, and readiness history.

The native apps live in `apps/prehab`. The dedicated API, background worker,
website/admin and this documentation site live under `hosted`.

Start with [local development](/guides/example/) or the repository's
`apps/prehab/README.md`. Historical product plans are preserved in
`docs/prehab/source`; they are not a promise that every roadmap item is implemented.
