TrainStack has two parts. The web app at [account.trainstackhq.com](https://account.trainstackhq.com)
holds your activities, analysis, training load, calendar, workouts and settings. The Ride app for
iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple Watch rides structured workouts on a smart trainer indoors, and sends
each ride to the web app.

## Signing in

Open the web app and press **Sign in with Apple**. TrainStack has no passwords and no other way to
sign in yet. Use the Apple ID you sign in to Ride with: TrainStack finds your account by your
Apple ID, so the same one reaches the same account from Ride and from the web.

The first time you sign in, TrainStack makes your account.

If signing in stops at **Sign in was cancelled.** or **Sign in did not finish. Try again.**, press
**Sign in with Apple** again. Apple's page has to finish and send you back for it to work.

## Where things are

| Page           | What it is for                                                                                                            |
| -------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Today**      | How ready you are, today's plan with a suggestion for each session, and today's check-in. Where you land after signing in |
| **Activities** | Everything you did, newest first, with this week's totals. Upload and log here                                            |
| **Training**   | Fitness, fatigue and form, and your bests                                                                                 |
| **Calendar**   | What you planned, what you did, and how closely they matched                                                              |
| **Workouts**   | Your workout library, the built-in workouts, lists, and the editor                                                        |
| **Settings**   | Weight, time zone, thresholds and zones, the calendar feed, and your data                                                 |

## A good first hour

1. [Get your activities in](/help/add-activities): upload files from your watch or bike computer,
   or sign Ride in with the same Apple ID.
2. [Set your thresholds](/help/thresholds-and-zones). Without them, TrainStack cannot score the
   load of rides, runs and swims, so they are left out of your fitness chart. (Strength and
   bouldering sessions get their load from how hard you rate them.) Once you have a few hard efforts in,
   TrainStack suggests thresholds from them.
3. Look at an activity's [analysis](/help/activity-analysis), then your
   [fitness and form](/help/fitness-and-form).
4. [Plan your week](/help/plan-your-week) on the calendar.

## What TrainStack does not do yet

TrainStack is young, and some things you might expect from other training apps are not there.
Each page says what is missing where it matters; in short:

- _No automatic import._ Activities do not arrive from Garmin, Strava, Apple Health, COROS,
  Wahoo, Suunto or Polar on their own. You upload their files, or ride with Ride. See
  [Getting activities in](/help/add-activities).
- _No outdoor recording._ Ride records indoor rides on a trainer. Runs and outdoor rides come
  from your watch or bike computer's files.
- _No training plans._ You plan each workout yourself. Nothing generates a plan or adapts one.
- _No sharing._ There are no friends, feeds or coaches. Only you can see your account, unless
  you give someone your [calendar feed](/help/calendar-feed)'s address.
- _No check-in in the app._ The daily check-in is on the web only.
- _Sign in with Apple only._ There is no email, Google or other sign-in.
- _No privacy policy or terms of service yet._ Until there are, do not rely on TrainStack for
  anything you cannot afford to lose, and [export your data](/help/your-data) now and then.

Something wrong or missing? Open an issue on
[GitHub](https://github.com/richard-better/trainstack/issues/new). If you use Ride, you can also
send feedback from its settings.
