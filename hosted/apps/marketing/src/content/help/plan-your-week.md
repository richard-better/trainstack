**Calendar** shows what you planned and what you did, day by day, with a summary for each week.

![The calendar, with planned workouts, what was done, and how closely](/help-images/calendar.png)

## Planning a day

Press the + button on a day ("Plan a workout, note or rest day"). Then choose:

- **Your workouts**: a workout from your library. Search by name or tag.
- **Built-in**: one of TrainStack's own workouts.
- **Note**: a line of text for the day, such as "Race day" or "Travel".
- **Rest day**: a planned day off, with a note if you want one.

A workout on the calendar is a copy. Editing it there with **Edit this copy** changes only that
day, and editing the workout in your library does not change days already planned. Each planned
workout shows its time and load, worked out from your thresholds on that day, and its profile.

Open a planned workout to see its steps, add **Notes** for that day (how to ride it, what to eat),
or change it.

## Moving, copying and skipping

Every item on the calendar has a menu (the ⋯ button on its card):

- **Move to another day**, or drag the card to another day. **Move with the keyboard** moves it with
  the arrow keys: Enter puts it down, Escape leaves it where it was.
- **Copy to another day** puts a second copy on another day.
- **Skip** marks a workout as skipped, with a reason if you want to note one, such as being ill.
  It stays on its day, so the week still shows what was planned. **Mark not skipped** undoes it.
- **Delete** removes it.

To repeat a week, open the week's menu (the ⋯ button at the end of its summary) and choose
**Copy this week to another**. Everything planned that week goes to the same days of the week you
pick, after anything already planned there.

## Planned next to done

Each planned workout shows its state:

| State   | Meaning                                                                 |
| ------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Planned | Today or later, and not done yet                                        |
| Done    | An activity on that day is paired with it                               |
| Skipped | You skipped it                                                          |
| Missed  | Its day has passed, nothing was paired with it, and you did not skip it |

Activities that no planned workout was paired with show as **Unplanned**.

TrainStack pairs an activity with a planned workout by itself when they are on the same day and
the same sport, for rides, runs and swims: it picks the closest in time and load. The card's edge
says how it was paired: **Matched automatically** or **Paired by you**.

If it paired the wrong one, open the planned workout, look at **Done as**, and press **Unpair**, or
pair another activity of that day. You can also use the menu on the calendar: **Pair with** an
activity, or **Unpair from** one. Strength, bouldering and other sports are never paired
automatically; pair them yourself.

Once paired, a workout gets a score of how closely it was done. See
[Planned and done: compliance](/help/compliance).

## The week's summary

Below each week, the summary adds up time, distance and load: done, against planned, such as
"6h 36m of 5h 25m". Your [check-ins](/help/check-in) show under each day too.

## Keyboard

**Month** and **Week** switch the view. The arrow buttons go to the previous and next month or
week, and **Today** comes back, or press ← and → and T.

## When a change does not save

The calendar saves each change straight away and keeps trying when you are offline. If you
changed the same thing on another device, the later change wins, and the calendar tells you which
it kept, with a way to put yours back.

## Not supported yet

- _No training plans._ TrainStack does not build a plan from a goal or race, or change your
  plan when you miss a session. You plan each workout yourself.
- _No coach._ Nobody else can plan your calendar.
- In Ride you can start, skip, move and unpair a planned workout, but you plan new ones on the web.
