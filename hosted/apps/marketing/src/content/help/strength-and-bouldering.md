Strength and bouldering have no file to upload, so you log them by hand, in the web app on your
phone between sets if you like. On **Activities**, open **Log session** and choose **Strength** or
**Bouldering**.

## Strength

Search for an exercise, or type a name that is not in the list and choose to add it as your own
exercise. For each exercise, **+ Add set** and enter the weight and reps, or the time for a hold.
A set can be **Working**, **Warm-up**, **Drop** or **To failure**, and can note reps in reserve and
the rest after it. Switch between **kg** and **lb** at the top; TrainStack keeps what you entered.
Bodyweight exercises count your weight from **Settings**, plus any weight you add.

**Superset with next** groups an exercise with the next one, done a set of each in turn.

## Bouldering

Choose **Gym** or **Crag** and name it. Pick the grade scale: **Font**, **V**, or your gym's
**Colours**, in the order the gym uses, each with the Font range it stands for if the gym gives
one. Then for each problem, pick its grade, how it went, and the attempts:

- **Flash**: topped on your first try, never tried before.
- **Send**: topped for the first time, after more tries.
- **Repeat**: topped again.
- **Project**: not topped yet.

## Finishing a session

Under **Session**, set the day, start time and minutes, and give it a name if you want. Rate
**How hard was the session?** from 0 to 10 once it is over, and press **Save session**.

The rating is what gives the session its load: an hour rated 5 counts like an hour at an easy-to-
moderate pace, an hour rated 10 more than an hour at threshold. Without a rating the session
counts no load toward your [fitness](/help/fitness-and-form). Heart rate is not used, because it
says little about how hard lifting and climbing are.

The session shows in **Activities** and on the calendar like any other. If your watch recorded it
too, upload the watch's file: TrainStack links the two as one workout, and the heart rate stays
with the watch's file.

## Progress

**Training** shows **Lifting and climbing** once you have logged a session:

- For each lift: your estimated one-rep max (Epley's formula, from the best set of 1 to 12 reps)
  and volume (reps times load, warm-ups left out). A session's page marks its personal records.
- For bouldering: your grade pyramid (first ascents and projects at each grade), and each session's
  hardest top.

## Not supported yet

- Logging in Ride or on the Apple Watch.
- Programs or routines to follow, and rest timers.
- Sport or lead climbing, and other sports logged by hand.
